## Cheat sheet

**Book value per common share**
- (Total equity − preferred claim) ÷ common shares outstanding
- Preferred claim = **liquidation value** (if given, otherwise par) + **cumulative arrears**, current year included
- Noncumulative preferred: no arrears, so deduct liquidation value only

**Property dividends**
- Measure at **FV on the declaration date**; ignore later FV changes
- Declaration: Dr Asset / Cr Gain (FV − CV), then Dr RE / Cr Property dividend payable (FV)
- Net RE effect = −carrying value

**Multiple-step income statement**
- Net sales = gross sales − discounts − returns; strip out any gains booked to sales
- Operating expenses: salaries, rent, insurance (only the period's portion), **depreciation**
- Other revenues and gains: interest, dividends (current-year only), gain on sale of equipment
- Other expenses and losses: interest expense
- Tax expense = rate × **pretax income from continuing ops**
- Discontinued ops below the line, **net of its own tax**
- Prior-year revenue goes through retained earnings, not this year's income statement

**Accounting changes**
- Change in principle (e.g., **from LIFO**): retrospective. Cumulative effect = **beginning** difference × (1 − t), booked to beginning RE of the earliest period presented
- **To LIFO**: usually prospective (retrospective is impracticable); disclose only
- Change in depreciation method: treated as a change in estimate, prospectively
- Error correction: prior-period adjustment to beginning RE, net of tax

**EPS and ratios (missed once each)**
- Splits and stock dividends are **retroactive** to Jan 1; no time-weighting
- Days sales in A/R = **ending** A/R ÷ (sales ÷ 365); A/R turnover uses **average** A/R

## Answers & explanations

1. **A.** Claim = 5,000 × $104 + 5,000 × $6 × 3 = $520,000 + $90,000 = $610,000. ($1,800,000 − $610,000) ÷ 50,000 = $23.80. B uses par; C ignores arrears; D ignores both.
2. **A.** +$40,000 gain (NI) − $340,000 dividend = −$300,000, which equals the carrying value.
3. **B.** Depreciation is an operating expense. A and D are other revenues and gains; C is other expenses and losses.
4. **A.** Continuing: $800,000 × 75% = $600,000. Discontinued: $200,000 × 75% = $150,000. B charges all the tax to continuing operations.
5. **A.** $3,000,000 − $90,000 − $40,000 − $25,000 = $2,845,000. The gain belongs under other gains.
6. **B.** $250,000 × 75% = $187,500, using the beginning difference. C uses the ending difference; D ignores tax; A nets the change during the year.
7. **B.** Restating LIFO layers is usually impracticable, so the change is applied prospectively and disclosed.
8. **B.** A change in depreciation method is treated as a change in estimate and applied prospectively; no restatement.
9. **C.** Restate for the 10% stock dividend: 330,000 × 12/12 + 66,000 × 9/12 − 30,000 × 2/12 = 374,500. A skips the restatement; D ignores the treasury purchase.
10. **Delta Corp**
    - a. Dr Land 30,000 / Cr Gain on land 30,000. Then Dr Retained earnings 150,000 / Cr Property dividend payable 150,000.
    - b. No entry.
    - c. Dr Property dividend payable 150,000 / Cr Land 150,000. The $155,000 FV is ignored.
    - d. −$120,000 (+$30,000 gain − $150,000 dividend), which equals the carrying value.
    - e. Claim = 8,000 × $55 + 8,000 × $2.50 = $440,000 + $20,000 = $460,000. ($2,400,000 − $460,000) ÷ 100,000 = **$19.40**.
